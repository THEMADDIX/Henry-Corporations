The UAE is the #1 destination for Indian exporters. With 0% personal income tax, a huge NRI consumer base, and booming e-commerce platforms like Amazon.ae and Noon, it's the easiest first international step for any Indian brand.
Overview

The UAE offers Indian businesses unmatched proximity, cultural familiarity and a 0% personal income tax environment. With over 3.3 million Indian expats and a population of 10 million high-income consumers, UAE marketplaces see strong demand for Indian food, fashion, beauty and electronics. Henry Corporation manages the complete UAE expansion lifecycle — from FTA VAT registration and UAE company formation to Amazon.ae and Noon seller account management.

Eligibility Requirements
  • Indian Private Limited, LLP or Sole Proprietor
  • Products permissible under UAE customs regulations
  • IEC (Import Export Code) from DGFT
  • Active GST registration preferred
  • Minimum 6 months business operating history
Documents Required
  • IEC Certificate
  • GST Registration Certificate
  • Company Incorporation Certificate
  • Bank Statement (6 months)
  • Passport copy of directors
  • Product catalogs / HSN codes
Setup Process
  1. 01Free export readiness assessment and UAE market analysis
  2. 02UAE FTA VAT registration (if applicable)
  3. 03Amazon.ae / Noon seller account creation and KYC
  4. 04Product listing, Arabic localisation and content creation
  5. 05Customs HS code mapping and import duty calculation
  6. 06Launch, account health monitoring and monthly VAT filing
VAT / Tax

5% UAE VAT — FTA registration required for sellers above AED 375,000 annual turnover. Voluntary registration available from Day 1. Henry Corporation files quarterly UAE VAT returns on your behalf.

Logistics

Air freight from Indian airports to Dubai / Sharjah is 2-3 days. Sea freight from JNPT/Nhava Sheva is 7-10 days. FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon UAE) and 3PL warehousing options available.

Timeline & Pricing
4–6 weeks end-to-end From ₹25,000 setup + ₹8,000/mo

The UK is the world's 4th largest e-commerce market with GBP-denominated sales and massive appetite for ethnic food, fashion, handicrafts and wellness products from India.
Overview

Post-Brexit UK presents a unique expansion opportunity for Indian exporters — a premium English-speaking market with its own customs regime, high purchasing power and strong demand for Indian products. Henry Corporation navigates UK VAT registration (Making Tax Digital compliance), Amazon UK seller setup, and cross-border customs documentation including UK EORI numbers.

Eligibility Requirements
  • Valid IEC from DGFT
  • Products complying with UK product safety standards
  • UK EORI number (we assist in obtaining)
  • UK VAT registration (for taxable supplies above £90,000 threshold or voluntary)
  • CE/UKCA marking where required
Documents Required
  • IEC Certificate
  • GST Certificate
  • Company registration documents
  • Product compliance certificates (CE/UKCA)
  • UK EORI application details
  • Bank statements (6 months)
Setup Process
  1. 01UK market feasibility assessment and product compliance check
  2. 02UK EORI registration and VAT registration (MTD)
  3. 03Amazon UK / eBay UK seller account setup and verification
  4. 04Product listing with UK-localised content and pricing
  5. 05Royal Mail / DHL / FedEx logistics setup from India
  6. 06Launch, performance monitoring and quarterly VAT filing
VAT / Tax

20% UK VAT. MTD (Making Tax Digital) compliance mandatory. Sellers above £90,000 annual turnover must register; voluntary registration available. Henry Corporation handles UK VAT returns on an MTD-compliant platform.

Logistics

Air freight from India to Heathrow/Gatwick: 3-5 days. Sea freight (LCL): 18-22 days. Amazon UK FBA available for B2C fulfilment. UK bonded warehouse options for B2B.

Timeline & Pricing

Germany is Europe's #1 e-commerce market by revenue. Amazon.de, Otto, and Zalando attract millions of high-income shoppers actively seeking quality products from emerging market brands.
Overview

Germany combines Europe's highest e-commerce spending with strong consumer protection laws and strict regulatory compliance. Henry Corporation manages German VAT (Umsatzsteuer) registration, §19 UStG threshold assessment, packaging compliance (VerpackG registration), Amazon.de account management and LUCID packaging register enrollment — making German market entry smooth for Indian exporters.

Eligibility Requirements
  • IEC and GST registration
  • Products compliant with EU CE marking requirements
  • German VAT registration (or EU OSS scheme registration)
  • LUCID packaging register enrollment (mandatory for B2C)
  • WEEE registration for electronic products (if applicable)
Documents Required
  • IEC Certificate
  • GST Certificate
  • EU CE/conformity declarations
  • LUCID registration confirmation
  • Company incorporation documents
  • Product safety data sheets
Setup Process
  1. 01EU and Germany-specific compliance and CE marking assessment
  2. 02German VAT registration or EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) registration
  3. 03LUCID packaging register enrollment
  4. 04Amazon.de / Otto seller account setup
  5. 05German-localised product listings with SEO
  6. 06Launch and ongoing VAT filing (monthly or quarterly)
VAT / Tax

19% German VAT (Umsatzsteuer). EU OSS scheme available for multicountry EU selling. Reduced 7% rate applicable for books, food and certain categories. Henry Corporation handles all German VAT filings.

Logistics

Air freight India to Frankfurt: 4-6 days. Sea freight (LCL): 20-25 days. Amazon.de FBA available; German 3PL warehousing recommended for cost efficiency.
