How to Sell on Amazon UAE from India: 2026 Complete Setup Guide

By Henry Corporation May 21, 2026 13 min read

Amazon UAE (Amazon.ae) is one of the fastest-growing e-commerce markets in the world, with GMV growing at 22% YoY and a shopper base increasingly comfortable buying from Amazon's vast product catalogue. For Indian sellers, Amazon UAE represents a unique opportunity: geographic proximity (5-day sea freight, 2-day air freight), a large Indian diaspora audience, strong demand for Indian-origin products (Ayurveda, textiles, handicrafts, food), and CEPA-driven duty advantages. But the setup is more complex than it looks. This guide covers everything you need to know to launch successfully in 2026.

Why Amazon UAE Is a Strong First International Market for Indian Sellers

Before we get into the how, let us address the why. Most Indian sellers who go international think USA first — it is the world's largest Amazon market, but also the most competitive, most expensive to enter, and hardest to win on ranking. Amazon UAE offers a different risk-reward profile:

  • Proximity advantage: Air freight from Mumbai or Delhi to Dubai takes 24–48 hours. Sea freight via Nhava Sheva takes 4–6 days. This means fast, affordable restocking and lower FBA stranded inventory risk.
  • India-UAE CEPA: The Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (signed 2022, operational 2022–ongoing) gives Indian goods preferential tariff access to UAE. Many product categories now have 0–5% import duty, making Indian sellers more price-competitive than Chinese counterparts.
  • Indian diaspora: 3.5 million Indians live in the UAE. They actively seek Indian brands, Indian food products, and culturally familiar goods.
  • Lower competition: Amazon UAE has roughly 1/10th the seller density of Amazon USA. Ranking organically is far more achievable.
  • Gateway to GCC: Amazon UAE listings are visible to shoppers across UAE, Saudi Arabia, and other GCC countries. Saudi Arabia's e-commerce market alone is expected to reach $30 billion by 2027.

Do You Need a UAE Company to Sell on Amazon UAE?

This is the most common question Indian sellers ask — and the answer is nuanced:

Technically, no. Amazon allows foreign entities (Indian companies) to register as Amazon UAE sellers. Your Indian company's documents are acceptable during account registration.

In practice, strongly recommended. Here is why:

  • UAE VAT registration: Amazon UAE is required to collect UAE VAT (5%) on transactions. If your annual UAE sales exceed AED 375,000 (mandatory threshold) or AED 187,500 (voluntary threshold), you must register for UAE VAT. VAT registration requires a UAE entity (company or branch).
  • FBA agreement: Amazon's fulfillment agreement for UAE warehouses prefers/requires a UAE-established entity for certain product categories.
  • Banking: Receiving AED disbursements from Amazon requires a UAE bank account or an accepted multi-currency account (Payoneer, Wise Business). A UAE company makes opening a proper UAE business bank account far easier.
  • Credibility: UAE customs documentation for FBA inventory shipments is smoother with a UAE consignee entity.

Our recommendation: Set up a UAE Free Zone company (IFZA, SHAMS, or RAKEZ are cost-effective for e-commerce operations) alongside your Amazon UAE account. Total Free Zone setup cost: AED 12,000–18,000. See our UAE business setup guide for details.

Step-by-Step: How to Start Selling on Amazon UAE from India

Product Research & Validation

Use Jungle Scout, Helium 10, or Amazon's own Product Opportunity Explorer (available in Seller Central) to identify underserved categories on Amazon UAE. Focus on: products with 10+ reviews per top listing (healthy demand), fewer than 15 strong competitors on Page 1, and products where Indian-origin adds perceived value.

Indian product categories with strong organic demand on Amazon UAE: Ayurvedic skincare, herbal supplements, Indian ethnic wear, brass home décor, natural spices and superfoods, cotton bedding, and Bollywood/Indian cultural items.

Set Up Your UAE Entity and VAT Registration

Register a UAE Free Zone company — IFZA (International Free Zone Authority), SHAMS (Sharjah Media City), or RAKEZ are popular for e-commerce with competitive licensing fees. Process takes 7–14 working days. Once incorporated, register for UAE VAT with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA) — the registration is free and online. You will receive your TRN (Tax Registration Number) within 5–20 working days.

Register as an Amazon UAE Seller

Go to sell.amazon.ae and click "Start Selling." Choose the Professional selling plan (AED 180/month, ~$49). During registration you will need: business name and address, business registration documents (UAE company or Indian company), national ID / passport of the account owner, bank account details (UAE bank or Payoneer/Wise), credit card for fee payment, and UAE VAT TRN (if registered).

The verification process typically takes 3–7 days. Amazon may request a video verification call to confirm your identity — prepare your documents in advance.

Create Your Product Listings

For each product, create a listing with: a keyword-rich title (include primary Arabic + English keywords), 5 bullet points covering benefits and specifications, a detailed product description, and high-quality product images (minimum 1000 x 1000 px, white background for main image). Use Helium 10 or MerchantWords to research Arabic keywords — a significant portion of Amazon UAE searches are in Arabic.

If your product already sells on Amazon India, you can link your existing listings via the Amazon global account linking feature. However, repricing and compliance review for UAE market standards is still required.

Ship Inventory to Amazon UAE FBA Warehouse

Create an FBA shipment in Seller Central → Inventory → Send to Amazon. Amazon will assign you warehouse addresses in Dubai (typically the Dubai South or JAFZA fulfillment centers). Prepare your shipment: each unit must have a unique FNSKU barcode (printed or stickered), cartons must meet Amazon's weight/dimension requirements, and you need a commercial invoice and packing list for UAE customs clearance.

Shipping options from India: (a) Air freight — 2–3 days, higher cost (~₹200–350/kg); (b) Sea freight LCL — 7–12 days, lower cost (~₹40–80/kg), suitable for non-urgent large shipments; (c) Courier (DHL/FedEx) — fast, expensive, suitable only for small initial test shipments.

Handle UAE Customs & Import Compliance

All goods entering the UAE go through Dubai Customs or the relevant emirate's customs authority. Required documents: Commercial Invoice (in AED or USD), Packing List, Certificate of Origin (India — for CEPA benefits, get this from FIEO or your local Chamber of Commerce), Bill of Lading / Airway Bill, and Import Declaration (your UAE freight forwarder handles this). Customs duty on most products is 5% ad valorem; with India-UAE CEPA, many categories have 0–2% preferential duty.

Price Your Products Competitively

Price research: check Amazon UAE directly for current prices. Consider: product cost + India freight + UAE customs duty + FBA fees (storage + fulfillment) + Amazon referral fee (5–15% depending on category) + VAT (5% collected from customer, remitted to FTA). Your target: at least 25–35% net margin after all costs. Indian sellers often have a cost advantage of 15–25% over Chinese sellers in UAE due to CEPA duty preferences and proximity freight savings.

Launch with Amazon Advertising (PPC)

When you launch a new listing, your organic rank is zero — you need PPC to get initial visibility and reviews. Start with Sponsored Products Auto campaigns (let Amazon identify keywords), then move to Manual campaigns targeting the best-performing keywords from your auto campaign report. Budget: AED 2,000–5,000/month for the first 3 months to build organic rank. Target ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sale) of 20–35% during launch; optimize toward 12–18% once ranked.

Manage VAT Compliance & Quarterly Returns

Amazon UAE collects VAT (5%) from customers on your behalf and remits it to you as part of your disbursement. You must then file quarterly UAE VAT returns with the FTA. The return reports your output tax (VAT collected on sales), input tax (VAT paid on UAE purchases/services), and net VAT payable. For Indian sellers with a UAE company, this is straightforward with basic bookkeeping. Non-compliance penalties start at AED 1,000 for late filing.

Common Mistakes Indian Sellers Make on Amazon UAE

  • Skipping Arabic keywords: A significant share of UAE shopping searches are in Arabic. Listings without Arabic content in the title or backend keywords miss these shoppers entirely.
  • Ignoring VAT compliance: Many Indian sellers treat UAE VAT as an optional formality. The FTA actively audits marketplace sellers, and penalties for non-registration or non-filing are significant.
  • Wrong product selection: Choosing categories already dominated by Amazon UAE private labels or heavily funded Chinese sellers. India's advantage lies in authenticity-driven categories (Ayurveda, handicrafts, organic food) where Chinese sellers cannot compete on product story.
  • Over-sending FBA inventory: Sending 6-months' worth of stock on Day 1. Start with 30–60 days of inventory until you have real velocity data. High FBA storage costs from slow-moving stock destroy margins.
  • Not using Sponsored Brands: Once you have at least 3 products in the same category, Sponsored Brands ads (header banner ads) dramatically increase brand awareness and conversion. Many Indian sellers on Amazon UAE only use Sponsored Products and miss this entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sell on Amazon UAE from India without a UAE company?

Technically yes — Amazon allows non-UAE entities to register as sellers. However, Amazon UAE requires UAE VAT registration for sellers above the threshold, and VAT registration is much simpler with a UAE entity. For serious Amazon UAE operations, a UAE Free Zone company (AED 12,000–18,000) makes banking, VAT compliance, and FBA logistics significantly easier.

How much does it cost to start selling on Amazon UAE from India?

A realistic startup budget: Amazon Professional account (~AED 180/month), UAE Free Zone company (AED 12,000–18,000 one-time), UAE VAT registration (free), initial FBA inventory shipment (AED 5,000–20,000 depending on product), and Amazon PPC budget (AED 2,000–5,000/month). Total first-year investment: approximately AED 35,000–60,000 (₹8–14 lakh).

What products sell best on Amazon UAE?

Top-performing categories on Amazon UAE include electronics & accessories, fashion & apparel, home décor, beauty & personal care (including Ayurvedic/herbal), kitchen appliances, baby products, and fitness equipment. Indian-origin products in Ayurveda, textiles, and handicrafts perform particularly well due to the large Indian diaspora audience and CEPA tariff advantages.

Is FBA or FBM better for Amazon UAE sellers from India?

FBA is strongly recommended. It gives Prime eligibility, 1–2 day delivery, and significantly higher Buy Box win rate. FBM from India (5–15 day delivery) results in very low conversion rates against FBA-listed competitors. The only exception is very large/heavy products where FBA economics are prohibitive.

How does India-UAE CEPA benefit Amazon UAE sellers?

The India-UAE CEPA gives Indian-origin goods preferential import duty rates at UAE customs — typically 0–2% instead of the standard 5% most-favoured-nation rate. To claim CEPA benefits, you need a Certificate of Origin (Form AI) issued by FIEO or your local Chamber of Commerce, submitted with your import declaration. This reduces your landed cost in UAE compared to Chinese sellers by 3–5%, which is significant at Amazon's margin levels.

Can I link my Amazon India account to Amazon UAE?

Yes. Amazon's Global Selling program allows you to manage Amazon India and Amazon UAE (and other Amazon marketplaces) from a single global account dashboard. You can share inventory data and listings, though you need to price, translate (for Arabic), and optimize listings separately for each marketplace.
